Block 3.1: CEO Dashboard - Key Metrics

Comprehensive dashboard for executive leadership with core KPIs: revenue, orders, regional growth, and marketing channel efficiency. All data updates in real-time to enable informed business decisions.

Block 3.2: Customer behavior analysis dashboard

Comprehensive investigation of customer patterns and lifetime value. Top 5% of customers generate a significant portion of revenue. Analysis of repeat purchases reveals that customers with 2+ orders demonstrate the highest value. On average, a customer places 4.8 orders with a ~30-day interval between purchases. Identification of at-risk customers: they spend above average but haven't purchased in 300+ days – this segment is priority for re-engagement campaigns.
